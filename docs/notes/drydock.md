# The drydocks (MAP.BIN DOCK00-DOCK07) and the PSM0 map mesh

## What they are (2026-11-15)
- `MAP.BIN` entries 787-802 are **eight drydocks**. For dock n (0-7): entry **787 + 2n** is the map mesh (`PSM0`), entry **788 + 2n** the same dock as a `PSC1` model whose name is `DOCKnn`. They are map-table records 67-73 (DAT entry 4; record 66 is the neighbouring map) and all share one tile list: MAP entries 803-810 (eight 256 x 256 `SHTXST` texture atlases).
- PSM0 and PSC1 have the same extent (**PSM0 float coordinates x 4096 = PSC1 vertex units**) and mostly the same vertices (DOCK00: 3,382 of the PSC1's 3,903 are also PSM0 vertices; the PSM0 has about 3% more faces), so the PSC1 is used for drawing and `psc1.py` reads it as is.
- Size grows with n (DOCK00 3,404 faces, DOCK07 5,651), so **DOCK07 is called "fully built"**: an assumption (the files do not say). DOCK07 shows two floating piers, a long building row and a large olive ship hull.
- Atlas choice: low three bits of the last byte of a face's UV-record attribute bytes (values 0-4, bit 6 another flag); the third attribute byte (0 / 127 / 128 / 255) is unknown (blend / alpha?). Checked by eye only. The piers' deck faces show a white and black grid (the railing / fence region of atlas 2): maybe a wrong atlas or alpha handling, not confirmed.
- Water level: the flat-area histogram puts decks at z 2,048 and 7,424; sea at **z = 0** is used (`waterZ`).
- The DOBJ000-049 / DOCKJ00-70 models in CHR (cranes, towers, buildings) are separate scene objects; **no layout for them was found** (no table of their file ids in the executable or DAT; MAP dock meshes have no object table), so they are not placed.

## Composite and viewers
- `tools/build_drydock.py` (called by `tools/build_composite.py`) splits each dock by atlas into parts (one texture per part) and writes `web/models/composite_drydock_<n>.bin`, entries in `composites.json` ("Drydock (fully built)" = n 7, "Drydock stage 1-7"), and the atlases as `web/models/dock_tile_<k>.png`.
- Viewers: the Models page (Composite section) and `web/drydock.html` (dock picker, smooth water, palette, wireframe, save picture).

## PSM0 format, partly decoded (little-endian)
- Header: `'PSM0'`, then 15 u32 section offsets: [1] 0x40, [2] vertices, [3] faces, [4] colours, [5] UV records, [6] 0, [7..11] small / unknown tables, up to the object table at the end for ordinary maps.
- **Vertices** (section [2]): u16 count, then 16-byte records from +2: f32 x, y, z, and a u16 pair (counter, 0). Units: 1.0 = 4096 PSC1 units.
- **Faces** (section [3]): u16 count, then 36-byte records: u16 f0 (index, unknown), u16 v0..v3 (vertex indices, a triangle when v2 == v3), u16 flags (low byte 0x14, bits 8-9 = atlas 0-3, 0x4000 another flag), u16 colour index (< colour count), u16 UV record index (< UV count), three s16 -1 (neighbours?), 14 more bytes (zeros, a 1 and 0xbf00: a normal or flags).
- **Colours** (section [4]): u16 count (1,282 in DOCK00), then 3-byte colours. **UV records** (section [5]): u16 count (1,343), 12-byte records = four (u8, u8) corner coordinates + a u32 (6, 4, 3 ...: atlas / mode).
- Not decoded: sections [7] to [11], the grid / cell index the engine probably uses, and any object table (none found in the dock meshes).

## Update (second pass, 2026-11-15)
- **Face colours matter.** Each face has a colour (3 bytes, 128 = neutral) from the colour table; the dock's lighting is baked into them (mean about 80 of 128, i.e. darker than the plain texture). `tools/build_drydock.py` now writes `composite_drydock_<n>.col` (3 bytes per face, in part order) and `web/drydock.html` multiplies the texture by them, unlit, with a Brightness slider (the game's ambient light is not known). The Models page composite view still ignores them (shows the plain texture).
- **The "chequered piers" were transparency, not a wrong atlas**: atlas 2 has 16,831 fully transparent texels (alpha 0); my first contact sheet pasted the PNGs ignoring alpha, which showed those texels white. The pier decks are cream deck panels with open cells you see the sea through, plus dark rails and posts.
- **Slot 1 of each dock PSC1** (the part with `lod` 0 0 0) holds the dock's **basin water**: 58 flat faces at z 1986 in DOCK07 (all 29 or 58 faces in DOCK00 / 01 / 04 / 07; DOCK02 / 03 / 05 / 06 also have 6-18 extra non-flat faces at other heights, now included as parts). The composite keeps the flat faces as their own part ("Basin water"), and the open sea is drawn at z 0 (the piers' decks sit just above it).
- **DOBJ / DOCKJ placement: searched again, still not found.** Tried: references to their file ids in every archive (BEV, EVT, DAT, PRV, EFC; only noise), a table of their resource indices (641-761, consecutive) in the executable (none), the 16-byte object-table pattern in the dock PSM0 (none; the tail table at header[13] is 240 mostly empty 28-byte rows, not objects), model-name strings in other archives (only the eight DOCK maps themselves). They probably have no entity rows (no names in the entity table), so scripts load them directly.

## Update: atlas choice fixed (2026-11-16)
- The earlier PSC1 rule (low 3 bits of the UV record's last attribute byte) gave only atlases 0-4 and put cream open-cell panels on the pier decks. The map mesh (PSM0, entry 787 + 2n) has its own UV records whose **byte 8 (low 3 bits) = atlas 0-7** (values seen 0, 1, 3, 5, 6, 7). Building from the PSM0 with that rule gives solid grey concrete decks and a pale grey hull, matching in-game screenshots. Corner UVs are u8 / 256. `tools/build_drydock.py` now uses the PSM0 route by default (`DOCK_PSC1=1` = old route); 2 of DOCK07's 5,718 faces have an out-of-range UV or colour index and are skipped.
- Also: the viewer now draws the dock textures without sRGB decoding (the PS2 multiplies raw texture by raw face colour) and the default Brightness is 1. Basin-water faces are no longer split into their own part (only checked by eye that nothing looks wrong).

## Update: dock menu code read (2026-11-16)
- The dock menu module (about 0x1cfc00-0x1d0400) reads the dock type as `now_dock_typ / 6` and sub = `% 6` (byte at 0x2ae766), then loads: UI textures (DSN 35, 44-46, 116+: SHTXFS sheets), and **behaviour scripts by BEV id 8000 + entry**: 12 + type + 8 x sub (0x1f4c + ...), 66 + type, 74 + type, 82 + type (576-byte scripts, 3 groups of 8), 90 + type (8 stage files of 5-6.5 KB, they contain mission codes such as PAC_E_140), and 8091 + type.
- BEV 12-58 (1.8-1.9 KB, 48 scripts = 8 types x 6 sub) start with a small header and then pairs of records (kind 5, kind 6) numbered 2-7. Kind 5 holds three float triples (x, y, z; z 4-5), kind 6 a target triple (z about 1) and 0.8. All coordinates fall inside the dock's volume in PSM0 float units (1 = 4096 model units), and the triples change smoothly with sub. These look like **camera paths** (camera position waypoints plus look-at target, six shots), not object placements. Scripts for neighbouring entries differ by only a few words.
- So the cranes and buildings are still not located. Their loader is not in the menu module (it loads only 2D sheets and these scripts); it is probably in the 3D scene code the menu calls, not examined.

## BREAKTHROUGH (2026-11-16): the dock scenery placement table
- **Where**: PSM0 header offset index 12 (`struct.unpack_from('<15I', d, 4)[12]`) = a table of u16 count (288) + u16 0 + 28-byte rows: f32 x, y, z (map units; x 4096 = model units, same frame as the dock mesh), u32 id, u32 flag, u32 extra, u32 0. Earlier I had called this the "mostly empty 28-byte rows" and the object table at index 14 (empty in docks) was the wrong one.
- Rows 100-257 with flag 1 (or 2) are the scenery objects: **id & 0xffff = 1000 + DOCKJ number** (DOCKJ00-DOCKJ61, CHR entries 420 ...), id >> 16 = a variant 0-6 (probably facing; not decoded, the viewer uses 90 degrees x variant). Counts grow with the dock: DOCK00 39 objects (10 kinds), 01 37, 02 51, 03 44, 04 43, 05 78, 06 99, 07 111 (41 kinds): the dock gains buildings, towers, cranes and warehouses as the story advances. Rows 0-24 are bounds / camera / water points, 224-225 and 238-239 (flag 2) and 283-287 (flag 0, z 0.15) other markers, not decoded.
- **Scale**: DOCKJ vertex units x about 16 give the dock's map scale (x12 also looks plausible; the viewer has a slider, default 16). Not measured.
- **How I found it**: `MCB1.BLN` (488 MB) + `MCB0.BLN` (index, 12-byte records id / offset / size): pre-built memory blocks. Ids 0x8000-0x802f = the 48 dock variants (counter 0-47; `DOCK TYP%d-%d MCB`), 0x90xx 84 other blocks, 0x49xx the mission maps, 0x40xx / 0x43xx-0x47xx others. Each block is a sequence of chunks: u32 archive type (0 MAP, 1 CHR, 5 DAT, 7 BEV, 9 DSN), u32 byte offset of the entry in that archive's .BIN, u32 stored size (multiple of 2048), then 12-byte-offset LZ data (the game's decompressor, `tools/wg2.py`), next chunk at +12+size. A dock block therefore lists exactly which models (DOCKJ props and their textures), maps, BEV camera scripts and DAT tables that dock loads. The DOCKJ numbers loaded match the placement table's kinds. Scratch tools: mcb9.py (walk), mcb10.py (entries).
- **Not in the table**: DOCKJ19, 20, 22, 23, 41 (the 5"/38 gun mount), 46, 60, 61 (46, 60, 61 appear in docks 2-7 but are flat plates). DOCKJ41 is loaded by every dock block yet has no row: its placement (the two mounts on the big grey hull) must come from elsewhere (hardpoints or a script).
- Tools: `tools/build_dock_objects.py` -> `web/models/dock_objects.json`; `web/drydock.html` places the objects (Objects button, Object size slider).
- Light sprites (user's insight): the lamp / floodlight props (DOCKJ17 and friends, shared atlas) have flare cards whose texture is black around a white glow, black = nothing. The viewer splits every prop's triangles by whether their UV corners fall on dark texels and draws those additive (black adds nothing), which turns the black squares into glows and the black spike (DOCKJ42) into a light beam.
- Orientation (2026-11-16): the table stores no yaw. The high 16 bits of the id (0-6) are not a facing: one prop type along one pier takes several values yet is always aligned with the pier, so the viewer no longer turns props by it (it did, 90 degrees per step, and the user saw every part misoriented). Props are drawn unturned with a global Turn selector (0 / 90 / 180 / 270). Pier-aligned props are not all authored the same way (long axis along y for DOCKJ33, 36, 37, 39, 50-52; along x for DOCKJ24, 49, 54), so a per-model facing probably comes from the model itself or from the engine. Lights (additive glow triangles) are off by default (Lights button).
- Facing, user comparison (2026-11-16): the large buildings (DOCKJ00-14, the group at the top of the dock) must be turned 90 degrees counter-clockwise seen from above; the viewer now does this (pier props stay unturned). The white streak beside the helipad (DOCKJ42) is also in the user's in-game daytime screenshot, so it is not a light (maybe a spray) and stays visible.

## Vehicle / mount spawns in the dock's BEV script (2026-11-16)
- BEV 90 + n (n = dock type 0-7, 5-6.5 KB) holds **object spawn records**: 12-byte header `0a 0s 00 00 | 24 00 00 00 | 44 11 00 00` (s = 2-9, a running number), then f32 x, y, z (map units, same frame as the placement table), f32 rx, ry, rz (radians, rz is the yaw), f32 type (10.0 or 20.0), f32 0, u32 -1; each is followed by a kind-1 instance record repeating the position, and for moving ones a path of x, y, 0.5 waypoints (BEV 90 at 0x8ac etc.: along the piers at deck height). A dock has 8 spawns (eight BEV scripts, 8 types x 8).
- Type 20 spawns: (-5.345, -6.0, 0.5) and (-5.345, 2.0, 0.5) in every dock (these coincide with PSM0 rows 256-257 whose field holds 0x140000), plus 2-3 on the platform top (z 4.7) and the hull (z 2.6) in docks 0-3. Type 10 spawns: z 0.5 (two with a path, the 'moving' ones: (-5.131, 12.039) yaw -1.57 and (-7.732, -12.0) yaw 1.57), z 1.07-1.10 on the hull deck at x 12-18 or x -14 to -20, z 2.9-4.2 on the platform; the one at (9.424, 20.349, 2.929) coincides with PSM0 row 225 (flag 2).

Moving props are drawn at their start. BEV 12-64 and 89 each hold one more spawn of type 10 at about (-1.0, -0.07, 0) yaw -1.57 (probably the player's ship).
- No type other than 10 and 20 occurs, so the other loaded props (cranes DOCKJ15, lamps 19, 22, 23, trucks) must be placed by other records; not found. `tools/build_dock_objects.py` now exports these spawns (guess=1) and removes the earlier row guesses for DOCKJ41 / DOCKJ20.

- **Light faces are flagged in the model data (2026-11-16)**: in the DOCKJ PSC1 models, slot 12 holds the props' night variant; its faces with face-flag bit 0x100 (0x104, 0x105, 0x108, 0x109, 0x10c) and a non-zero 4th UV attribute byte (128 / 136; body faces have 0) are the lights: floodlight cones and flares (DOCKJ17), truck headlight beams (DOCKJ19, 22, 23, 32 faces each), spotlight beams and glare cards (DOCKJ14, 42-48, 60, 61). `tools/export_dock_lights.py` writes just those faces (`web/models/<id>_L.bin`, `dock_lights.json`); the dock page and `web/dockprops.js` draw them additively, off by day, and no longer guess lights from dark texels. The ordinary (flag 0x8) faces of slot 12 duplicate the body and are not drawn. Whether the game depth-tests these faces against the scene is not in the data; the viewers draw them with the depth test on and no depth write (the usual setting for additive effects), so nearer geometry cuts them off.
