Water generator

The game's sea is two small indexed tiles per group, recoloured with one of 16 alternate palettes. This builds an endless smooth sea from them: the palette lookup is done in the shader so the colours blend smoothly instead of showing blocky pixels, several scaled, rotated and drifting copies are mixed so the tiling does not show, and a few waves move the surface. How the game itself moves the tiles is not known, so the motion is a choice. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
